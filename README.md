# lars simulator lol

funny 3d horror game where a guy named lars chases you around a procedurally generated backrooms maze. fix the generators, try not to blink at the wrong time, and whatever you do, don't let lars catch you.

## objective

1. fix all 4 generators (literally just hold `E` on them).
2. keep an eye on lars, blinking at the wrong time can be dangerous.
3. use your torch, crouch, sprint and jump to survive.
4. if the lights go out, find the fusebox and restore the power.
5. run to the EXIT door and escape.

## controls

* **`WASD`** - move
* **`Mouse`** - look around (click the game to lock mouse)
* **`Shift`** - sprint for your life
* **`C`** - crouch
* **`Space`** - jump (yes, you can jump over lars)
* **`Q`** - blink (hold to keep your eyes shut)
* **`RMB`** - squint / focus
* **`Z`** - look over your shoulder
* **`E` (Hold)** - fix gens u noob
* **`E`** - interact with the fusebox
* **`T`** - toggle torch
* **`[` / `]`** - adjust FOV
* **`F`** - fullscreen

## how to run

open the game through the GitHub Pages website, or literally just download `index.html` and double click it.

**sound on.** lars is a lot easier to locate when you can hear him. unfortunately, he can hear you too.
