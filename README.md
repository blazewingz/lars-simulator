# lars simulator lol

funny 3d horror game where a guy named lars chases you around a backrooms maze.

## objective

1. fix all 4 generators (hold `E` on them).
2. don't let lars eat your face.
3. run to the EXIT door and escape.

## controls

* **WASD** - move
* **Mouse** - look around (click game to lock mouse)
* **Shift** - sprint for your life
* **E (Hold)** - fix gens
* **Space** - blink (lars gets speedy when your eyes are closed, do NOT nap)

## how to run

literally just download `index.html` and double click it. sound on or lars will sneak up on u.
